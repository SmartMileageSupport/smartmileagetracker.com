Mileage tracking for taxes, answered.

How do I track mileage for taxes?

The IRS publishes standard mileage rates. For car expenses, Table 5-1 of IRS Publication 463 lists records showing, among other things, the mileage for each business use, the total miles for the year, the date, your business destination and the business purpose. Smart Mileage Tracker records the date, route, distance and addresses of each drive it detects, gives Business Purpose its own field, and exports the log as a CSV or PDF.

Does automatic mileage tracking drain my battery?

Smart Mileage Tracker uses your location in the background so drives can be detected automatically, and background GPS does use some battery. You can limit automatic tracking to when your iPhone is plugged in.

Is Smart Mileage Tracker really free?

It is free to download, automatic tracking is unlimited and free, and there is no account and no subscription. If paid extras ever arrive, they will be a one-time purchase, never a subscription.

Which drives have an IRS mileage rate?

The IRS publishes standard mileage rates for business, medical and charitable driving, and by default Smart Mileage Tracker prices all three at the IRS rate in effect on the date of each trip (built-in rates cover 2023 through 2026). Driving between home and a regular place of work is commuting and is not deductible (IRS Publication 463). IRS Notice 2026-10 says unreimbursed employee travel expenses can't be claimed as a miscellaneous itemized deduction; the Notice names a few groups who can still deduct them in figuring adjusted gross income. Medical mileage is deductible only if you itemize, and only to the extent your total medical expenses exceed 7.5% of adjusted gross income (IRS Publication 502).

Deduction figures in the app are estimates, not tax advice; confirm your situation with a tax professional.

Where does my trip data go?

Your trips are stored in a database on your iPhone. There is no account and no server of ours for them to be uploaded to. To show street addresses, the app sends trip coordinates to Apple's Maps service. You can back up your trips to a file you own. Privacy policy.
